The big picture — what training actually is

Imagine you are a chef learning to cook 196 dishes. You have a cookbook (the dataset) with 8,144 recipe photos. Training is the process of you cooking the same dishes over and over, tasting the result each time, and adjusting your technique based on how wrong it tastes.

epoch
Cooking through every recipe in the cookbook once. You do this 20 times (NUM_EPOCHS=20).
batch
Cooking 32 dishes at once (BATCH_SIZE=32) instead of one at a time. More efficient.
forward pass
Actually cooking the dish using your current technique. The result is your prediction.
loss
The judge tastes your dish and gives a score for how wrong it is. Lower is better.
backward pass
The judge tells you exactly which steps in your technique caused the dish to be wrong.
optimizer step
You adjust your technique slightly based on the feedback. Not too much — just a small nudge.
learning rate
How much you adjust your technique after each batch of feedback. Too big = overcorrect. Too small = never improve.
weights
Your current technique — all the specific muscle memory and habits you've built up. The model has 2M+ of these.
